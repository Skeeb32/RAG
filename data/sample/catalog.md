# Service catalog tool
The fictional demo organization has three services: atlas-api, beacon-worker and cedar-index. Service owners, service tiers and runbook identifiers live in a separate SQLite service catalog, not in this knowledge base. Ask the lookup_service tool for those structured records. The catalog is seeded from data/catalog.json during ingestion and can be reseeded for local demonstrations. It is not a live operational monitoring system. The model selects the tool through the native function-calling protocol. A failed lookup is reported, never silently replaced with guessed ownership data.
